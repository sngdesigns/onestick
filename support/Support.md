# One Stick — Support

Thanks for playing One Stick!

Need help with the game, a purchase, or something else? Email us at:

**onesticksupport@gmail.com**

We typically reply within 1–2 business days. Please include:

- the device you're using (iPhone / iPad),
- the app version (Settings → bottom of the page shows "Version X (Y)"),
- and a short description of the issue.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I restore my Remove Ads purchase?**
Open Settings → REMOVE ADS → expand the section → tap **Restore Purchases**.
This works on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

**How do I reset my progress?**
There is no in-app reset. Deleting the app removes your saved progress,
scores, and streaks from the device.

**Why do I see ads?**
The app is free and ad-supported. Ads are served in non-personalized mode.
You can remove them entirely with the one-time Remove Ads purchase.

**Where is my data stored?**
Game progress is stored only on your device. If you sign in to Game
Center, your score and achievements go to Apple's Game Center service.
See our [Privacy Policy](privacy/PrivacyPolicy.md).
